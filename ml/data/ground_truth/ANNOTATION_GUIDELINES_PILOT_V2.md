# Human Verification Annotation Guidelines — Pilot V2

## Overview & Purpose

This document provides explicit annotation guidelines for the 100-event Human Verification Pilot V2 (`pilot_v2`). The goal of this pilot is to obtain high-quality human ground-truth labels for candidate thermal events using multi-source satellite and GIS evidence.

---

## CRITICAL ANNOTATION RULES & SCIENTIFIC PRINCIPLES

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **1. AI-Assisted Labels are Hypotheses Only**
> Do NOT blindly follow the provided `ai_assisted_suggestion`. AI weak labels are generated by rule heuristics and exist solely to prioritize review and test candidate hypotheses. Annotators must evaluate all evidence independently.

> [!WARNING]
> **2. Proximity to Infrastructure is NOT Proof of Industrial Activity**
> Proximity to a facility, mine, refinery, or power plant alone does **not** prove that a thermal detection originated from industrial operations. Crop clearing, brush burning, or illegal dumping fires frequently occur adjacent to industrial property boundaries.

> [!WARNING]
> **3. Persistent Recurrence Alone is NOT Proof of an Industrial Source**
> High temporal recurrence (e.g. active days > 10 in 30 days) must be evaluated jointly with land cover, built-up fraction, and thermal intensity. Seasonal agricultural practices or recurring waste burning can mimic temporal persistence.

> [!NOTE]
> **4. Joint Evidence Evaluation**
> Annotators must evaluate land-cover evidence (forest fraction, cropland fraction, builtup fraction), temporal persistence (active days, event counts), thermal behavior (FRP magnitude, duration), infrastructure context (facility type, distance to mine/quarry/factory/refinery), and spatial context jointly.

> [!CAUTION]
> **5. Use `unknown_requires_verification` for Insufficient or Conflicting Evidence**
> If evidence is missing, conflicting (e.g. high forest fraction and high cropland fraction with no clear dominance), or inconclusive, classify the event as `unknown_requires_verification`. Do NOT force a class guess.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **6. Evidence & Fact Discipline**
> - Annotators MUST record the strongest specific evidence supporting their decision in the `evidence_notes` field.
> - Annotators MUST NOT infer facts that are absent from the supplied evidence package.

## Six-Class Taxonomy

Annotators must assign exactly one of the six standard classes:

1. **`persistent_industrial_source`**
   - *Definition*: Stationary, recurring thermal emission directly attributable to industrial plant operations (e.g. gas flaring, kiln, furnace, continuous stack emission).
   - *Key Evidence*: Distance to industrial facility $\le 2.0$ km, high temporal persistence ($\ge 10$ active days in 30d), high built-up fraction ($\ge 0.20$), stable moderate FRP.

2. **`industrial_fire_or_abnormal_event`**
   - *Definition*: Acute, high-intensity flare-up or industrial fire event occurring at an industrial site, refinery, chemical plant, or storage facility.
   - *Key Evidence*: High FRP flares ($\ge 150.0$ MW), short duration acute spikes, proximity to refinery/factory/chemical facility, or high built-up fraction ($\ge 0.30$).

3. **`wildfire_or_forest_fire`**
   - *Definition*: Open biomass fire occurring in forested, woodland, or uncultivated natural vegetation zones.
   - *Key Evidence*: High forest fraction ($\ge 0.40$), located away from industrial facilities ($> 2.0$ km), high FRP spikes, low built-up fraction.

4. **`agricultural_burning`**
   - *Definition*: Open burning of crop residue, stubble, or agricultural clearing fires.
   - *Key Evidence*: High cropland fraction ($\ge 0.40$), transient active days ($< 5$ days in 30d), distance to facility $> 2.0$ km, low-to-moderate FRP.

5. **`mining_or_other_industrial_activity`**
   - *Definition*: Open-pit coal/mineral mine fires, quarry thermal operations, overburden clearing, or localized mining equipment activity.
   - *Key Evidence*: Event located within active mine/quarry GIS boundary or near mine/quarry ($\le 3.0$ km) with non-forest land cover.

6. **`unknown_requires_verification`**
   - *Definition*: Ambiguous, conflicting, or insufficiently documented thermal event requiring high-resolution imagery or ground inspection.
   - *Key Evidence*: Equal or conflicting forest/cropland/builtup fractions, low FRP without facility context, or unresolvable spatial context.

---

## Confidence Rating Scale

For every assigned label, the annotator must assign a confidence level:
- **`HIGH`**: Multi-source evidence strongly aligns with a single taxonomy class without ambiguity.
- **`MEDIUM`**: Strong evidence supports the primary class, but slight ambiguity exists (e.g. mixed land cover or moderate distance to facility).
- **`LOW`**: Significant ambiguity or weak evidence, but leaning toward a primary class; consider using `unknown_requires_verification` if confidence is very low.

---

## Double-Annotation Protocol

- A 30-event subset is assigned independently to Annotator 1 and Annotator 2.
- Annotators work independently without viewing each other's labels.
- Inter-annotator agreement (Cohen's kappa) will be calculated upon completion.
- Disagreements will be reviewed by a lead annotator during formal adjudication.
